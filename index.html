Our beginning

A small court can hold a big future.

Jade Mafak grew from years of showing up for young people and grassroots sport in Edo State. Handball was the starting point: a place to build discipline, teamwork and self-belief.

Today, Jade Mafak Development Ltd is a structured platform for that same commitment. We combine sustainable enterprise with programmes in sports, education, mentorship, life skills and community engagement, creating opportunity that reaches beyond the final whistle.
