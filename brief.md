We are a Nigerian company passionate about human development, youth empowerment, grassroots sports, and community transformation. Guided by our motto, “Raising a People. Building His Culture,” we work to help individuals discover their potential, develop their abilities, and contribute meaningfully to society. Our approach combines sustainable business with social impact initiatives focused on sports, education, mentorship, life skills, and community engagement. Through these efforts, we aim to create opportunities for young people, nurture emerging talents, support underserved communities, and build partnerships that promote inclusive growth and sustainable development.

Our journey began long before the formal registration of Jade Mafak Development Ltd, rooted in years of personal commitment to grassroots sports, youth mentorship, and community development in Edo State, Nigeria. Through our involvement in handball development, we have worked to identify and nurture young talents, particularly children and young people from disadvantaged backgrounds, helping them develop discipline, confidence, teamwork, and a sense of purpose. Today, Jade Mafak Development Ltd represents the next chapter of that journey: transforming years of experience, sacrifice, and community service into a structured platform for greater impact. As we move forward, we seek to build strategic partnerships with organisations, institutions, businesses, and development agencies to expand our programmes, create opportunities, empower communities, and build a sustainable future for the next generation. We are building more than a company; we are building a platform for opportunity, growth, and lasting change.

Our vision is to become a leading platform for youth empowerment, grassroots sports development, and community transformation in Nigeria and beyond. We envision a future where every young person has the opportunity to discover their potential, develop their abilities, and contribute meaningfully to society. We see a future where communities are empowered, talents are nurtured, and sustainable development is achieved through strategic partnerships and impactful programmes. We are committed to building a platform that creates opportunities, inspires growth, and fosters lasting change, guided by our core values of integrity, excellence, teamwork, community service, and social impact.
